# Ideas: data, exploration and theory (2026-10-19)

Tim: "Brainstorm what additions you can do to the game with data, exploration, theory, etc. … Implement your good ideas."

| # | Idea | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Live wolf odds** for every living player from public info (setup, revealed cards, Seer claims), exact enumeration, shown on the voting grid | **Build — done** | The core odds layer, and it sits right where the vote happens |
| 2 | **Measured setup balance**: village win % from 10,000 simulated games per suggested setup, for each rule set | **Build — done** | Answers "is this fair?" with a number instead of a guess |
| 3 | **Setup lab chart**: village win % against player count for each setup family (Simple / Classic / Full / Rulebook) | **Build — done** | Shows at a glance where the rulebook tables go lopsided |
| 4 | **Point system vs simulation**: each setup's Ultimate Werewolf point total beside its measured win % | **Build — done** | A theory check: where do the printed points agree with play? |
| 5 | **Computers at three measured strengths** (Easy / Normal / Smart) with a village-vs-wolves win matrix | **Build — done** | Same "How strong?" idea as Cribbage |
| 6 | **"What really happened"**: after the game the log shows every secret night action (checks, guards, attacks, potions) | **Build — done** | The study/learn layer: see the hidden information after the fact |
| 7 | **Private odds** in vs-computer games: your own card, wolf partners and Seer results feed your odds | **Build — done** | The odds become *your* odds, not just the table's |
| 8 | **Why this %?** Tap the ? on the odds to see what they're built from, plus each player's odds with and without the claims | **Build — done** | Turns the number into something you can learn from |

| 10 | **Vote record**: after a vs-computer game, how often each player voted for a wolf | **Build — done** | Post-game analysis of who read the table well |
